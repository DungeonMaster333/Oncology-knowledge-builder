## Oncology Concepts to Learn

1. **Fludeoxyglucose F18 (FDG) & PET Imaging:** A radioactive diagnostic agent used in PET imaging. Because rapidly dividing cancer cells exhibit altered metabolism, FDG accumulates inside tumors, lighting them up on scans to reveal primary sites, lymph node involvement, and distant metastases.
2. **The Warburg Effect:** The biological mechanism underpinning FDG imaging. Cancer cells predominantly produce energy through high rates of aerobic glycolysis rather than oxidative phosphorylation, resulting in high glucose consumption.
3. **Institutional ANDAs in Nuclear Medicine:** Unlike standard tablets manufactured centrally, radiotracers labeled with radioisotopes (like Fluorine-18, which has a half-life of only ~110 minutes) often require specialized institutional or regional manufacturing setups approved via ANDAs to supply nearby imaging clinics.
4. **Keyword Noise in Competitive Intelligence (CI):** Regulatory data feeds frequently flag terms like "tumor" or "cancer" broadly (e.g., flagging TNF-alpha blockers like Cimzia for autoimmune disease or broad antiviral/antifungal filings). Analysts must distinguish true oncology indications from mechanistic name overlaps.
