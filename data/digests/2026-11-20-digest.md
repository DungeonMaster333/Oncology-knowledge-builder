## Scientific Research

### Senolytic Navitoclax Reverses T-Cell Mediated Anti-PD-L1 Resistance (PMID: 43056377)
1. **What happened:** A mathematical modeling study (validated with murine data) demonstrated that age-associated senescent CD8+ T cells drive resistance to anti-PD-L1 checkpoint blockade by secreting VEGF; adding the senolytic drug navitoclax selectively eliminates these senescent cells and extends remission.
2. **Cancer indication:** Cancer resistance to PD-L1 blockade.
3. **Drug/asset/company involved:** Navitoclax (ABT-263) and anti-PD-L1 therapy; Company: Not specified in source data.
4. **Why it matters:** Identifies immune senescence as a critical mechanism of immunotherapy failure and provides a rationale for timing senolytic combinations to restore anti-PD-L1 sensitivity.
5. **Source type:** Peer-reviewed scientific journal (*PloS ONE* / PubMed).

### GPCR-Gαq Pathway Drives Adaptive Resistance to Trametinib in Melanoma (PMID: 43056352)
1. **What happened:** Using *C. elegans* and human A375 melanoma models, researchers identified that upregulation of the GPCR PAR2 and Gαq-PLC-PKC signaling mediates adaptive resistance to trametinib; dual inhibition of MEK and Gαq signaling restored drug sensitivity and suppressed tumor growth in xenografts.
2. **Cancer indication:** BRAF-mutant melanoma.
3. **Drug/asset/company involved:** Trametinib; Company: Not specified in source data.
4. **Why it matters:** Uncovers a non-MAPK adaptive bypass pathway responsible for MEK inhibitor resistance and highlights a synergistic combination strategy.
5. **Source type:** Peer-reviewed scientific journal (*PLoS Genetics* / PubMed).

### Recombinant Oncolytic Vaccinia Virus (oncoVV-shHSP70) in PDAC (PMID: 43056033)
1. **What happened:** Researchers engineered a recombinant oncolytic vaccinia virus targeting HSP70 (oncoVV-shHSP70), which inhibited the NRF2/HO-1 pathway, elevated reactive oxygen species (ROS), induced apoptosis, suppressed tumor growth in nude mice, and increased CD8+ T-cell infiltration in immunocompetent mice.
2. **Cancer indication:** Pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC).
3. **Drug/asset/company involved:** oncoVV-shHSP70; Company: Not specified in source data.
4. **Why it matters:** Overcomes low replication limitations of standard oncolytic viruses to present a potential cytotoxic and immune-stimulating therapy for treatment-refractory pancreatic cancer.
5. **Source type:** Peer-reviewed scientific journal (*Medical Oncology* / PubMed).

### Sensory CGRP Inhibition Restores Immunity in Head and Neck Squamous Cell Carcinoma (PMID: 43056154)
1. **What happened:** Preclinical research revealed that sensory neuron hyperexcitability and calcitonin gene-related peptide (CGRP) drive immunosuppression (increasing Treg infiltration); genetic knockout of CGRP combined with radioimmunotherapy eradicated tumors, and high-dose psilocybin acted as a CGRP inhibitor to suppress tumor growth.
2. **Cancer indication:** Head and neck squamous cell carcinoma (HNSCC).
3. **Drug/asset/company involved:** Psilocybin, Clopidogrel, Radioimmunotherapy; Company: Not specified in source data.
4. **Why it matters:** Links the neuro-immune axis to cold tumor microenvironments and introduces CGRP signaling as an actionable target to overcome immunosuppression and radioimmunotherapy resistance.
5. **Source type:** Peer-reviewed scientific journal (*The Journal of Clinical Investigation* / PubMed).
