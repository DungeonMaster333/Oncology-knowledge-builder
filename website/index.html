Oncology Learning Engine

Build the fundamentals before going deeper into individual diseases.
Oncology Foundations
Start here.

Learn what cancer is, how normal cells become malignant, how tumors grow and spread, and why oncology is different from many other therapeutic areas.

Core concepts: carcinogenesis, mutations, tumor biology, metastasis, tumor heterogeneity and the tumor microenvironment.

Treatment Modalities

Understand the major treatment classes:

Surgery · Radiotherapy · Chemotherapy · Targeted therapy · Immunotherapy · Hormonal therapy · Cell & gene therapy

The goal is to understand not only what each modality does, but where it fits in the patient treatment pathway.

Biomarkers & Diagnostics

Learn how biomarkers influence diagnosis, prognosis, treatment selection and clinical-trial eligibility.

Key concepts include molecular testing, companion diagnostics, genomic alterations, PD-L1, HER2, EGFR, ALK, KRAS, BRAF and MSI/MMR.

Clinical Trials

Understand Phase I–III development, endpoints, inclusion/exclusion criteria, randomization, comparator arms and regulatory evidence generation.

Commercial lens: trial differentiation, standard-of-care comparator, line of therapy, biomarker population and probability of success.

Patient Journey

Map the patient from symptoms and diagnosis through staging, treatment selection, therapy, monitoring, progression and subsequent lines of therapy.

This is the foundation for understanding patient flow, treatment sequencing, unmet needs and market opportunity.

Pharma Commercial Lens

Translate clinical science into commercial intelligence.

Focus areas: patient population, epidemiology, diagnosis rate, biomarker testing, treatment rate, line of therapy, market share, treatment duration, persistence, switching, pricing, access, competition and pipeline.

Analyst Lens

Questions to ask whenever you read an oncology development.

Don't just ask “what happened?”

Train yourself to translate every oncology event into clinical, competitive and commercial implications.

  • Does this change the standard of care?
  • Which patient population is affected?
  • Which line of therapy is involved?
  • Does it expand or shrink the addressable market?
  • Does it create differentiation versus competitors?
  • What does it mean for the pipeline and future market structure?
